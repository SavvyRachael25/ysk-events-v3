      Where each dollar is allowed to go

      YSK needs money, but most grant money comes with an address on it. A lab grant buys the lab and nothing else. Only flexible money, the kind that comes from events, sponsors, members and a very few funders, can pay salaries or cover youth clinics. Every line below is a funder, drawn as thick as the ask, ending where that money is allowed to land. This map covers the grants goal and Patrons's fundraising lane together, because both pay for the same things.
